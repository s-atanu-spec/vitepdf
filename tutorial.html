Tutorial

Everything in VitePDF, step by step. It takes about five minutes to read.

1. Install

  1. Open the download page and click VitePDF-Setup-1.4.0.exe.
  2. Run the downloaded file.
  3. Windows may show a blue box, "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the file has no paid publisher certificate.
  4. Read the short terms, tick I agree, and click Install.

No internet and no admin rights are needed. VitePDF is added for your Windows account only. To remove it, use Settings, Apps, VitePDF, Uninstall.

2. Find VitePDF and pin it to the taskbar

After the install, three entries are in the Start menu: VitePDF, VitePDF Capture and VitePDF Scroll.

  1. Click the Start button, or press the Windows key.
  2. Type VitePDF. The three entries appear in the results.
  3. Right-click VitePDF Capture and choose Pin to taskbar.
  4. Do the same for VitePDF Scroll.

Both icons now stay on your taskbar. One click starts a capture, over a browser or any other program. Without typing, the entries are also under Start, All apps (shown as "All" on newer Windows 11), letter V.

3. Work on files with a right-click

  1. Select one or more pictures or PDFs in a folder.
  2. Right-click and choose VitePDF. On Windows 11, choose "Show more options" first if you do not see it.
  3. Pick the tool. The new file appears beside the original, under a new name. Your original is never changed.

4. VitePDF Capture: part of the screen as a PDF

  1. Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + P, or click VitePDF Capture in the Start menu or on your taskbar.
  2. The screen dims. Drag a box around what you want.
  3. The first time, choose where captures are saved: Downloads, Desktop or your own folder.
  4. The file is saved as vitepdf-capture.pdf. A second one becomes "vitepdf-capture (2).pdf".
Use Capture when everything you need is visible on the screen at once. Use Scroll when the page is longer than the screen.

5. VitePDF Scroll: a long page as one PDF

  1. Open the page and scroll to where the capture should begin.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + S, or click VitePDF Scroll in the Start menu or on your taskbar.
  3. Drag a box over the part of the page that scrolls. Keep menus and side panels outside the box.
  4. Move the mouse pointer into the red box and keep your hands off. After the countdown, the page scrolls by itself.

The red box is what gets captured. The thick line at the bottom is the END line.

  1. When the last part you want is on screen, touch the mouse. The scrolling stops.
  2. Drag the red END line up to the last line you want, or scroll a little by hand. Then click Finish.

The capture ends exactly at the END line.

  1. Check the preview. The badge shows how many pages you will get. Drag the red handles if you want to trim the start or the end.
  2. Click Save PDF. The file is saved as vitepdf-scroll.pdf.

Blue lines show where each page ends. "One long page" keeps everything on a single page.

Good to know
The toolbar can be dragged out of the way.
"Scroll by hand" lets you scroll yourself from the start. Go slowly and steadily.
Cancel stops the capture and saves nothing.
The text in this PDF is a picture: sharp, but it cannot be selected or searched.
In some programs the page does not scroll by itself. Use "Scroll by hand" there.

6. Check that your download is genuine

Every release is signed by the author. The key fingerprint is:

A88B A544 E686 3A3C 1877 0B0C 3C40 DEBC

The README explains how to check a download against it. Always check a result before you use it.
